Overview

Discover a Himalayan lake amidst snow-capped peaks

The Brahmatal Trek brings you close to some of the most impressive mountain landscapes in Uttarakhand. Set against the towering peaks of Mount Trishul and Mount Nanda Ghunti, it offers panoramas, peaceful alpine lakes, snowy trails and enchanting Himalayan forests.

The trail takes you through oak and rhododendron forests, open meadows and scenic ridges before reaching the serene Brahmatal Lake. In winter, snow often transforms the landscape into a white wilderness. Viewpoints such as Jhandi Top, and further on Brahmatal Top, bring expansive Himalayan scenery into view.

Brahmatal can be a rewarding choice for first-time Himalayan trekkers who have prepared adequately, as well as experienced hikers seeking winter mountain scenery. Snow, cold and altitude can make the route physically demanding.

Why go

5 reasons to fall in love with Brahmatal

1. Two beautiful high-altitude lakes

Bekaltal is tucked among thick oak forests with a calm, secluded atmosphere. Brahmatal Lake offers open terrain and dramatic Himalayan scenery — two distinct experiences on one trek.

2. Spectacular ridge walks

The ridge sections near Tilandi and Jhandi Top are among the most memorable parts of the trek. Trishul and Nanda Ghunti dominate the skyline, with valleys and distant villages adding depth — ideal for photography.

3. Views of the famous Roopkund region

Selected ridge viewpoints give a sense of the vastness of the mountains and the trekking routes that cross this part of Uttarakhand.

4. Enchanting oak and rhododendron forests

Towering oaks, winding woodland paths and rhododendron groves between Lohajung and Bekaltal. In spring the trees can burst into pink and red; in winter, bare branches and snow give the woods a different look.

5. Peaceful campsites surrounded by nature

The Gujreni campsite sits among forest and open clearings. As evening arrives it becomes a place to relax, share stories and appreciate the mountains.
