Overview

Kedarkantha: a journey to the snowy Himalayan summit

Kedarkantha is a popular Himalayan trekking destination in Uttarakhand, celebrated for its forest trails, scenic campsites and rewarding summit climb. Starting from villages in the Sankri region, the trek takes you through dense mountain forests, open clearings and high-altitude slopes before reaching the summit at approximately 12,500 ft (3,810 m).

What makes Kedarkantha appealing is the variety packed into a relatively short journey. In winter, snowfall can turn the landscape into white forests and snow-covered trails; in warmer seasons the same route reveals green woodland, open meadows and clear mountain views.

For people planning their first Himalayan summit trek, Kedarkantha can be a memorable introduction, provided they prepare properly for the altitude, cold and physical demands.

Why go

5 reasons Kedarkantha should be on your bucket list

1. Experience the charm of a Himalayan village

The journey begins with the warmth and simplicity of mountain life. Kotgaon, nestled in the Garhwal Himalayas, welcomes trekkers with traditional wooden houses, terraced farms and views of the surrounding peaks. The historic Someshwar Temple and distinctive Koti Banal-style architecture add to its cultural charm — a chance to connect with local traditions before the trek.

2. Walk through enchanting Himalayan forests

Towering pine, oak and other Himalayan trees form a natural canopy, with sunlight filtering onto winding paths. Winter brings a blanket of snow; other seasons reveal rich greenery. The crisp air and the sounds of the forest make every step rewarding.

3. Discover beautiful meadows and scenic campsites

Open meadows and forest clearings offer a refreshing contrast to the dense woodland. Bhoja Dhadi is especially memorable for its expansive mountain views and spectacular sunsets, and under a clear night sky camping becomes one of the highlights of the journey.

4. Enjoy a trek with scenic trail variations

Trail sections vary in terrain, scenery and difficulty — quiet forest paths, open ridgelines, snowy slopes and panoramic viewpoints. Route choices and itineraries may vary with trail conditions and weather.

5. Reach the summit and witness the panorama

An early start and a steady uphill climb make summit day the most challenging and rewarding part. At approximately 12,500 ft, sweeping views of snow-covered Himalayan peaks and distant ranges reward the effort.

Before you go

At a glance, best time & packing

Trek at a glance

  • Trek difficulty: Easy–Moderate
  • Duration: 5 Days 4 Nights
  • Highest altitude: 12,500 ft
  • Trek type: Summit Trek
  • Pickup & drop: Dehradun
  • Stay type: Guesthouse / Camps
  • Minimum age: 10
  • Maximum people: 30+

Best time to go

  • December to April — this is primarily a winter/snow trek
  • Late December–January for the deepest, freshest snow
  • March–April for a mix of snow and clearer green trails lower down

What to pack

  • Waterproof trekking shoes with ankle support
  • Down jacket + 3-layer clothing system
  • Woolen cap, gloves, and UV sunglasses (snow glare is real)
  • Trekking poles — genuinely useful on the summit descent
  • Headlamp for the pre-dawn summit push
