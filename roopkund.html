Roopkund Mystery Lake Trek

One of the Himalaya's genuine mysteries — a small glacial lake at 16,500 ft scattered with centuries-old human skeletons, still not fully explained. The trek there runs through some of Uttarakhand's most celebrated high-altitude meadows.

Overview

What this trek is actually like

The trail to Roopkund is really two treks in one: the first half through Bedni Bugyal, regularly ranked among India's most beautiful high-altitude meadows, and the second half a genuinely demanding push through scree and snowfields to the lake itself at 16,500 ft.

The skeletons — over 600 sets of human remains dating back roughly 1,200 years — were first documented in 1942 and remain a subject of ongoing archaeological study. Their origin is still debated, which is part of what makes standing at the lake such a strange, quiet experience.

Before you go

Best time & what to pack

Best time to go

  • Late May to June — snow still present, best for the "true" Roopkund experience
  • September to mid-October — clearer skies and stable weather post-monsoon
  • Avoid the monsoon months (July–August) due to landslide and trail-wash risk

What to pack

  • Full winter layering system even in the May/June window — Bhagwabasa camp is genuinely cold
  • Gaiters — snowfields near the lake are common even outside peak winter
  • A solid pair of broken-in trekking boots (this is not a trek to test new shoes on)
  • Trekking poles, non-negotiable for the scree sections
  • High-SPF sunscreen and glacier-rated sunglasses
