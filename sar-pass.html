Overview

Forest trails, alpine meadows and snow-covered mountains

The Sar Pass Trek is an exciting Himalayan adventure in the Parvati Valley of Himachal Pradesh. It offers dense forests, charming mountain villages, open meadows and snow-covered slopes along a single journey.

The trek commonly begins in the Kasol region, beside the Parvati River — known for its relaxed atmosphere, scenic walking trails, cafés and range of accommodation. Before starting, you can explore the local market, enjoy the riverside and arrange last-minute essentials.

Forest paths lead to open clearings and alpine meadows, while higher sections reveal expansive mountain views. During the snow season, parts of the trail may be covered in snow. As the days go on, the surroundings become quieter and more remote.

Sar Pass can be considered by beginners with good basic fitness who are prepared for sustained walking, uphill climbs and changing weather. Snow can make some sections more demanding, so physical preparation, suitable equipment and an experienced team matter.

Why go

What makes the Sar Pass Trek special?

A diverse Himalayan experience

The landscape changes as you gain altitude — from lively Kasol into quieter forests, traditional settlements and open alpine terrain.

A more adventurous high section

Steep ascents and potentially snowy trails bring a more adventurous atmosphere as you climb.

Crossing the pass

Crossing the pass is the defining moment of the trek, rewarding your effort with expansive mountain scenery.

Forest trekking, camping and a high-altitude crossing

For travellers who want all of these on one trip, Sar Pass offers a memorable introduction to Himalayan adventure.
