Founder's story

Adventure Saathi started with a simple complaint: most trips felt like they were run for a spreadsheet, not for the people on the trail.

After years of watching trekking groups get treated like line items — rushed itineraries, guides who didn't know the route, zero communication once you'd paid — the idea was to build something smaller and more honest: a company where the person planning your trip actually knows the mountain, and stays reachable long after the booking confirmation lands in your inbox.

That's still the whole model. Every route on this site has been walked by someone on our team before it's sold to you. Every WhatsApp message is answered by a person, not a bot. And every batch stays small enough that a guide can actually learn your name.
