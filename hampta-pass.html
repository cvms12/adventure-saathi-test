Overview

A trek where two landscapes meet

Hampta Pass is a remarkable journey through the changing landscapes of Himachal Pradesh. The trail begins in the green valleys around Manali, passing through forests, grassy meadows and mountain streams before climbing into a much more rugged high-altitude environment.

As you approach the pass, the greener Kullu side slowly disappears behind you while the dry, rocky mountains of Lahaul begin to dominate the horizon. Within a short distance the scenery changes dramatically — from lush vegetation and flowing streams to vast barren slopes and expansive Himalayan skies.

The combination of forest trails, open valleys, stream crossings, high-altitude terrain and the spectacular pass crossing makes Hampta Pass one of Himachal Pradesh's most memorable trekking experiences. It is about experiencing the dramatic transformation of the Himalayas with every step.

Why go

5 reasons to put Hampta Pass on your trekking list

1. The beautiful journey from Manali to Jobra

The adventure starts before you begin walking. Apple orchards, dense Himalayan forests and winding mountain roads make the drive part of the Hampta Pass experience.

2. Exciting stream crossings along the route

Mountain streams cross different sections of the trail, and their water level changes with the weather. Trained trek leaders help participants choose the safest route across.

3. The dramatic moment at Hampta Pass

Once you cross over, the green valleys of the Manali side are replaced by the dry, rocky landscape of Lahaul — the defining moment of the trek.

4. Campsites with completely different landscapes

Jwara is surrounded by greener mountains, Balu Ka Ghera has a rugged high-altitude look, and Shea Goru presents a broad valley with barren mountain surroundings.

5. Impressive peaks, glaciers and mountain valleys

Depending on route and weather, trekkers can see peaks such as Indrasan and Deo Tibba, along with dramatic rocky ridges and glacial features.

Before you go

At a glance, best time & packing

Trek at a glance

  • Trek difficulty: Moderate
  • Duration: 5 Days 4 Nights
  • Trek distance: Approx. 26 km
  • Highest altitude: Approx. 14,000 ft
  • Maximum people: 30
  • Stay type: Camping / Tents
  • Fitness level: Good basic endurance required
  • Age guidance: 10+ years
  • Pickup & drop point: Manali
  • Luggage / cloakroom: Subject to availability at the starting point
  • Trek equipment: Trekking equipment and safety essentials as specified in the package

Best time to go

  • Mid-June to September — the pass is snow-free and river crossings are manageable
  • July–August is peak monsoon in Kullu, so trails can be wetter and slippery on the lower stretches
  • September offers the clearest skies and driest trail conditions

What to pack

  • Sturdy waterproof trekking shoes (river crossings are frequent)
  • A change of dry socks and shoes for camp
  • Trekking poles — essential for the scree descent off the pass
  • Warm layers for Balu Ka Ghera and Shea Goru nights (can drop below freezing)
  • Sun protection — high-altitude sun on the Lahaul side is intense

Hampta Pass reaches approximately 4,270 metres (14,010 feet). Proper acclimatisation, a steady pace and awareness of altitude sickness symptoms are important.
