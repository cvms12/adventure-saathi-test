Overview

Discover the spiritual charm of Puri

Discover the spiritual charm of Puri, home to the revered Shree Jagannath Temple. This pilgrimage is an opportunity to seek the blessings of Lord Jagannath, explore Odisha's sacred heritage, enjoy the peaceful coastline and visit remarkable temples and cultural landmarks nearby. Puri is one of the four principal Char Dham pilgrimage destinations in Hindu tradition.

Located along Odisha's Bay of Bengal coast, Puri brings together centuries-old temple traditions, religious celebrations, local culture and beautiful seaside views. The centrepiece of the journey is Shree Jagannath Temple, known for its distinctive Kalinga architecture and deeply rooted rituals.

With Adventure Saathi, the trip can be planned as a short temple visit or an extended pilgrimage with nearby destinations, depending on your time and preferences.

Why go

5 reasons why you should visit Jagannath Puri

1. Experience deep spiritual peace

One of India's most revered pilgrimage destinations — a place to pray, reflect and find peace away from the routine of everyday life.

2. Seek the blessings of Lord Jagannath

A visit to Shree Jagannath Temple is a deeply meaningful experience, with the devotion, traditional rituals and sacred atmosphere strengthening your connection with your faith.

3. Discover rich religious traditions

Puri is known for its centuries-old customs, temple ceremonies and the world-famous Rath Yatra festival, giving a deeper appreciation of India's spiritual heritage.

4. Enjoy a peaceful escape by the sea

Beyond its religious significance, Puri offers a beautiful coastal setting and a slower pace of travel.

5. Create meaningful memories with family

Travel with family, share prayers, discover local traditions and spend quality time together — spiritual fulfilment, cultural exploration and memorable experiences.
